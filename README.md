Thyroid disorders, particularly hypothyroidism, can significantly impact mental and physical health. In this project, we explore a machine learning-based approach to predict thyroid disease using clinical data. The Decision Tree Classifier was chosen for its interpretability and high performance in medical data analysis.

According to performance evaluations, the model achieved:

Training Accuracy: 100%

Validation Accuracy: 97%

These results highlight the potential of decision trees in medical prediction tasks and may assist researchers in developing improved diagnostic systems for thyroid-related disorders.
